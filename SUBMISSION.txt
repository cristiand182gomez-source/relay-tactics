PITCH
Relay Tactics is a compact 2–4 player turn-based strategy game where you grow a connected network, contest rotating beacons and cut rival branches while managing energy. Browser players and HTTP agents share the same room code, with optional built-in bots, clear scoring, a match log and a scrubbable replay; the repository includes a documented JSON API and a runnable sample bot. Complete public 2/3/4-player matches and every replay state were verified over HTTPS; the Free demo host can wake slowly and resets rooms when it restarts.

LIMITS
Free demo hosting can sleep and loses rooms on restart, redeploy or sleep.

this is casual play without accounts or stakes. A small policy comparison
found first-seat advantage; exhaustive strategic balance is not claimed.
